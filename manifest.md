**Status:** Active team manifesto across **Claude Code**, **ChatGPT Codex** and **Antigravity**.

The **[master coordination instructions](https://github.com/ahuelsmann/MOBAflow/blob/main/.github/instructions/master-coordination.instructions.md)** are the detailed rule set in the product repository. Use the [MOBAflow GitHub Kanban Board](https://github.com/users/ahuelsmann/projects/1) ([KANBAN.md](https://github.com/ahuelsmann/MOBAflow/blob/main/docs/KANBAN.md)) and the linked `MOBAflow` issues and Draft PRs for live slice assignments, risk classes, slice teams and locks. `ahuelsmann/MOBAdev` manages only versioned AI configuration.

## Build as a multi-model agent network ("Quality, Quality, Quality")

- **Role != single model.** Roles define domain responsibilities and survive model updates. Every role and slice team draws on four capability profiles built strictly from the [approved AI models catalog](docs/allowed-models.md):
  - **`P1` Depth, Architecture & Safety (`Opus 5.5` max ceiling — in Claude Code `MOBAi Claude Opus`, or Antigravity `MOBAi Antigravity Opus` on the separate Google AI plan):** concurrency, Z21 protocol safety, runtime invariants, COO steering, independent review.
  - **`P2` Repo, Build & Fact Precision (`GPT-6.2 Sol` and `GPT-6.2 Astra` workhorse sub-agents in ChatGPT Codex `MOBAi ChatGPT Codex`, or `GPT-OSS 120B` failover in Antigravity):** solution/build graph (`Moba.slnx`), Spec Kit consistency, CI/Sonar/mutation verification, CPM/TD steering, cross-model review and Sites delivery.
  - **`P3` System Context, Product & UI/UX (`Gemini 4 Carbon` in Antigravity `MOBAi Antigravity Gemini`):** large multi-file context, WinUI 3 and MAUI interaction, Light/Dark themes, TrackPlan visuals, clear reader copy (`MOBAnews` and `docs/wiki/`).
  - **`P4` Cadence, Triage & Fast Delivery (`Sonnet 5.5` and `Gemini 4 Hydrogen`, plus `Claude Haiku 4.5` strictly for Claude Code read-only `Explore` searches and `GPT-6.2 Astra` for Codex sub-agent routines; `Claude Fable 5.2` prohibited):** rapid routine slices, test/doc additions, triage and status monitoring. Never use `Claude Fable 5.2` (maximum Claude tier is `Opus 5.5`).
- **`Opus 5.5` Quota-Splitting (Dual-Plan Shield):** Keep `MOBAi Claude Opus` (Anthropic plan) focused on lean COO intake/board steering, while routing **`Fresh Independent P1 Final Reviews`** and bulk `Opus 5.5` work by default to Antigravity's `Opus 5.5` pool (`MOBAi Antigravity Opus` / fresh Antigravity `P1` sub-agents on the separate Google AI plan). Keep the Kanban **`Ready`** column focused on the top **`2–3` prioritized slices** ready for immediate pickup (`Planned` holds the remaining backlog).
- **Cross-family diversity.** Writer and companion reviewer come from different model families (Claude writer -> Codex `P2` companion; Codex writer -> Claude `P1` companion). At Ready, every slice (`H`, `M` and `L`) receives a fresh independent `P1` (`Opus 5.5`) review (never the writer's or companion's session, defaulted to Antigravity's `Opus 5.5` pool) plus cross-family `P2`/`P3` verification so at least two model families verify the final comparison.
- **Risk classes (`H / M / L`).** High-risk (`H`: Z21, runtime, concurrency, persistence, ESP32, runtime/protocol dependencies) and medium-risk (`M`: UI, features, TrackPlan, speech, MOBAnews, UI/feature dependencies) slices run with a multi-model slice team from the design note on; low-risk (`L`: docs, wiki, test/tooling-only dependencies, <=3-file non-behavioral fixes) slices state intent in the PR body without a separate design note or per-commit companion, and still pass the fresh `P1` + cross-family final review at Ready. Review-fix rounds always keep the risk class of their slice.

## Agree on the work before writing code

- The maintainer owns product goals, priorities, material UX or architecture decisions, new costs and app, device or hardware authorization. The **Decision & Steering Triad** (`P1` COO + `P2` CPM/TD + `P3` Product/UX) steers slice cutting, risk classification and the design Go; the **COO** is the sole technical writer on the board and manages Ready and auto-merge. The CPM/TD can veto a new slice or lock within 30 minutes under the board rules.
- **Kanban `Planned` → `Ready` Maintainer Start-Trigger:** Moving a `MOBAflow` issue from **`Planned`** to **`Ready`** on the GitHub Kanban board is the maintainer's explicit pull-trigger authorizing the Decision & Steering Triad (`MOBAi Claude Opus` / `COO` intake) to pick up the issue. `MOBAi Claude Opus` (`COO`) is the sole intake owner: before starting, it checks Code WIP (`< 3`), active locks, and specification readiness; if clear, it immediately moves the issue to **`In progress`** (preventing duplicate pickup), cuts slices, forms the cross-model team, and opens Draft PRs. **3-World Parallel Track Distribution:** When Code WIP (`< 3`) allows multiple disjoint slices, distribute tracks simultaneously across all 3 active quota pools rather than serializing them in one tool (`Claude`: Backend, Host, Architecture & COO steering; `Codex`: `MOBAdemo/app` React/TypeScript, CI/Build/Specs & `P2` reviews; `Antigravity`: `MOBAflow` WinUI/XAML/Themes, `TrackPlan`, `Class L` Wiki/Help & `P3` UI/UX reviews). Keep epic umbrella issues distinct from active single-slice issues so `In progress` accurately reflects real WIP. Issues labeled `needs-specification` or requiring open product/UX/architecture decisions start with analysis and a concrete recommendation for the maintainer instead of blind coding.
- Cut work into independently mergeable slices. Each slice has one concern, one risk class (`H`, `M` or `L`), one named branch writer and one Draft PR. Aim for about 25 files or 400 changed lines at most, subject to the documented exceptions.
- Reserve the affected areas on the COO board before starting. Respect existing locks. At most three code slices can be open and two local agents can build at once. Documentation-only slices are outside the code limit and lock the files they edit.
- For class `H` and `M` slices, post a short design note (10–20 lines: goal, affected files, approach, risks and tests). Reviewers from complementary profiles (`P1`/`P2`, plus `P3` for UI/product work) check the note before the COO or Solution Architect gives the design Go. **Pre-Approved Plan-Slice Fast-Start (`Class M`):** Slices whose specification, locked paths, and acceptance tests are already fixed inside an approved Spec Kit `plan.md` (such as `#430` or `#510` slices) may start directly in a Draft PR with TDD (`Commit 1`: failing acceptance tests -> `Commit 2`: implementation), embedding the short design note in the PR body for companion code review without waiting on a separate pre-code prose round (`Class H` safety slices and unspecified features still require Design Go before code). Write a failing acceptance test first for new product behavior. Keep a user-visible feature that spans several slices behind a default-off toggle until complete.

## Deliver small changes to main with review from the start

- While a class `H` or `M` slice grows, the writer pushes small commits early (tests first, then implementation) and the cross-family companion reviewer records per-commit findings as PR review comments.
- Before every push that hands work on, merge the current remote `main` into the slice branch. Resolve conflicts in the writer's worktree and rerun the affected checks. Shared slice branches use merges and regular pushes, never rebase or force-push.
- The COO moves a green Draft to Ready after checking CI and current SonarCloud findings, then dispatches a fresh independent `P1` (`Opus 5.5`) review (default: Antigravity `Opus 5.5` pool) alongside cross-family `P2`/`P3` verification on the exact head and base. The writer fixes findings; changes to reviewed content require another independent review at the slice's risk class.
- **Disjoint `main`-Sync Fast-Path (No Redundant LLM Re-Review):** Once a slice already holds both `Fresh Independent P1 PASS` (`Opus 5.5`) and `Cross-Family P2/P3 PASS` on its slice diff, merging `origin/main` strictly to resolve a `BEHIND` branch state does **not** invalidate existing approvals or require another LLM review cycle — provided `origin/main` did not modify any file inside the PR's `LockedAreas` or diff and `git diff origin/main...HEAD` for the slice itself is unchanged (`0` conflict edits in slice files). Re-verify only CI, SonarCloud, and applicable mutation checks on the synced head, promote Draft -> Ready, and arm Squash Auto-Merge immediately.
- The COO arms squash auto-merge for the reviewed head once required checks, SonarCloud and the applicable mutation checks meet the documented conditions and no blocking findings remain. Disarm before a new push (unless performing a Disjoint `main`-Sync Fast-Path merge on an already-reviewed PR). Close the slice and release its locks after merge, recording `docs/` and `docs/wiki/` impact in the PR.
- **Branch & Worktree Hygiene:** Immediately after a PR merges (or worktree work is abandoned cleanly), remove the slice worktree, delete its local branch, ensure the remote feature branch is deleted (`--delete-branch`), and run `git fetch --prune`. Only `main` and active open-slice / standing monitor branches remain checked out or on remote.

## Keep coordination traceable

- **GitHub-as-Mailbox & CLI Channels:** GitHub issues and PRs are the single coordination mailbox — every design note, review verdict, and blocker MUST be posted immediately on the GitHub issue/PR (never left solely inside a local tool chat thread) and announced via the CLI channels (`codex.exe queue`, `claude.exe`, `agy.exe`) when handing off across tools. Track live slice status on the [MOBAflow GitHub Kanban Board](https://github.com/users/ahuelsmann/projects/1).
- **4 standing Root Head sessions (`MOBAi`), runtime task agents auto-archived:** The cockpit operates with **4** standing monitoring/steering sessions: **`MOBAi Claude Opus`** (`COO` `P1` / `Opus 5.5` in Claude Code), **`MOBAi ChatGPT Codex`** (`Technical Director & CPM` `P2` / `GPT-6.2 Sol` in ChatGPT Codex), **`MOBAi Antigravity Gemini`** (`Head of AI Strategy` `P3` / `Gemini 4 Carbon` in Antigravity), and **`MOBAi Antigravity Opus`** (`Acting COO & Fresh P1 Opus Orchestrator` `P1` / `Opus 5.5` in Antigravity). Regular intake starts in `MOBAi Claude Opus` (`COO`). All task-scoped execution, review and specialist agents (`Writer`, `Companion Reviewer`, `Final Reviewer`, `Head of UI/UX`, `Head of MOBAnews`, `Security`, `DevOps`) are spawned automatically at task runtime with their assigned profile model (`P1`–`P4`) and archived or removed (along with their worktree) immediately upon task completion (`claude rm` / `--no-session-persistence`, `codex archive`, ephemeral `agy --agent` invocations).
- If the same finding survives two failed fix attempts (in companion or final review), or an exchange exceeds ten agent messages on one topic, the COO triggers the circuit breaker and takes the smallest decision needed to the maintainer with a recommendation.
- The **Head of AI Strategy** (`P3` / `MOBAi Antigravity Gemini` in Antigravity) guards this `manifest.md`, evaluates orchestration improvements with measurable quality impact, and keeps all sanitized agent, rule and workflow definitions backed up in `ahuelsmann/MOBAdev`.
