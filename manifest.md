**Status:** Active team manifesto across **Claude Code**, **ChatGPT Codex** and **Antigravity**.

The **[master coordination instructions](https://github.com/ahuelsmann/MOBAflow/blob/main/.github/instructions/master-coordination.instructions.md)** are the detailed rule set in the product repository. Use the [MOBAflow GitHub Kanban Board](https://github.com/users/ahuelsmann/projects/1) ([KANBAN.md](https://github.com/ahuelsmann/MOBAflow/blob/main/docs/KANBAN.md)), linked `MOBAflow` issues, active task branches, and the local `AISwarmX Swarm Bus` (`mobai-swarm-bus`) for live slice assignments, risk classes, slice teams, and overlap-checked path locks. `ahuelsmann/AISwarmX` manages only versioned AI configuration.

## Build as a multi-model agent network ("Quality, Quality, Quality")

- **Role != single model.** Roles define domain responsibilities and survive model updates. Every role and slice team draws on four capability profiles built strictly from the [approved AI models catalog](docs/allowed-models.md):
  - **`P1` Depth, Architecture & Safety (`Opus 5.5` max ceiling — in Claude Code `AiSwarmX Claude Opus`, or Antigravity `AiSwarmX Antigravity Opus` on the separate Google AI plan):** concurrency, Z21 protocol safety, runtime invariants, COO steering, independent review.
  - **`P2` Repo, Build & Fact Precision (`GPT-6.2 Sol` and `GPT-6.2 Astra` workhorse sub-agents in ChatGPT Codex `AiSwarmX ChatGPT Codex`, or `GPT-OSS 120B` failover in Antigravity):** solution/build graph (`Moba.slnx`), Spec Kit consistency, CI/Sonar/mutation verification, CPM/TD steering, cross-model review and Sites delivery.
  - **`P3` System Context, Product & UI/UX (`Gemini 4 Carbon` in Antigravity `AiSwarmX Antigravity Gemini`):** large multi-file context, WinUI 3 and MAUI interaction, Light/Dark themes, TrackPlan visuals, clear reader copy (`MOBAnews` and `docs/wiki/`).
  - **`P4` Cadence, Triage & Fast Delivery (`Sonnet 5.5` and `Gemini 4 Hydrogen`, plus `Claude Haiku 4.5` strictly for Claude Code read-only `Explore` searches and `GPT-6.2 Astra` for Codex sub-agent routines; `Claude Fable 5.2` prohibited):** rapid routine slices, test/doc additions, triage and status monitoring. Never use `Claude Fable 5.2` (maximum Claude tier is `Opus 5.5`).
- **`Opus 5.5` Quota-Splitting (Dual-Plan Shield):** Keep `AiSwarmX Claude Opus` (Anthropic plan) focused on lean COO intake/board steering, while routing **`Fresh Independent P1 Final Reviews`** and bulk `Opus 5.5` work by default to Antigravity's `Opus 5.5` pool (`AiSwarmX Antigravity Opus` / fresh Antigravity `P1` sub-agents on the separate Google AI plan). Keep the Kanban **`Ready`** column focused on the top **`2–3` prioritized slices** ready for immediate pickup (`Planned` holds the remaining backlog).
- **Cross-family diversity.** Writer and companion reviewer come from different model families (Claude writer -> Codex `P2` companion; Codex writer -> Claude `P1` companion). At Ready, every slice (`H`, `M` and `L`) receives a fresh independent `P1` (`Opus 5.5`) review (never the writer's or companion's session, defaulted to Antigravity's `Opus 5.5` pool) plus cross-family `P2`/`P3` verification so at least two model families verify the final comparison.
- **Risk classes (`H / M / L`).** High-risk (`H`: Z21, runtime, concurrency, persistence, ESP32, runtime/protocol dependencies) and medium-risk (`M`: UI, features, TrackPlan, speech, MOBAnews, UI/feature dependencies) slices run with a multi-model slice team from the design note on; low-risk (`L`: docs, wiki, test/tooling-only dependencies, <=3-file non-behavioral fixes) slices state intent in the slice commit and `mobai-swarm-bus` handoff without a separate design note or per-commit companion, and still pass the fresh `P1` + cross-family final review before Master merge. Review-fix rounds always keep the risk class of their slice.

## Agree on the work before writing code

- The maintainer owns product goals, priorities, material UX or architecture decisions, new costs and app, device or hardware authorization. The **Decision & Steering Triad** (`P1` COO + `P2` CPM/TD + `P3` Product/UX) steers slice cutting, risk classification and the design Go; the **COO** is the sole technical writer on the board and manages Kanban `Ready`/`In review` transitions, review-gate promotion, and Master merges. The CPM/TD can veto a new slice or lock within 30 minutes under the board rules.
- **Kanban `Planned` → `Ready` Maintainer Start-Trigger:** Moving an issue from **`Planned`** to **`Ready`** on the GitHub Kanban board is the maintainer's explicit pull-trigger authorizing the Decision & Steering Triad (`AiSwarmX Claude Opus` / `COO` intake) to pick up the issue. `AiSwarmX Claude Opus` (`COO`) is the sole intake owner: before starting, it checks Code WIP (`< 3`), active `bus_locks`, and specification readiness; if clear, it immediately moves the issue to **`In progress`** (preventing duplicate pickup), cuts slices, forms the cross-model team, reserves `bus_locks`, and creates isolated task branches/worktrees. **N-World & Multi-Pool Parallel Track Distribution:** Distribute disjoint slices simultaneously across any **`N` active repositories / workspaces** (`workspace_id` isolated in `swarm.config.json` and `swarm-bus-v2.db`, e.g., `mobaflow`, `aiswarmx`, `sentix`, or future repos) and across all active quota pools rather than serializing in one tool (`Claude`: Backend, Host, Architecture & COO steering; `Codex`: `MOBAdemo/app` React/TypeScript, CI/Build/Specs & `P2` reviews; `Antigravity`: `MOBAflow` WinUI/XAML/Themes, `TrackPlan`, `Class L` Wiki/Help & `P3` UI/UX reviews). Keep epic umbrella issues distinct from active single-slice issues so `In progress` accurately reflects real WIP. Issues labeled `needs-specification` or requiring open product/UX/architecture decisions start with analysis and a concrete recommendation for the maintainer instead of blind coding.
- Cut work into independently mergeable slices. Each slice has one concern, one risk class (`H`, `M` or `L`), one named branch writer and one dedicated task branch (in its own worktree). Aim for about 25 files or 400 changed lines at most, subject to the documented exceptions.
- **Mandatory `bus_start_work` / `bus_locks` (`workspace_id`):** Reserve the affected paths atomically via `bus_start_work` / `bus_locks` (`AISwarmX.SwarmBus.Mcp` with explicit `workspace_id`) and on the COO board before editing shared or multi-writer workspaces. Respect existing foreign locks. At most three code slices can be active and two local builders can build at once per repo. Documentation-only slices are outside the code limit and lock the files they edit.
- **5-Point Pre-Flight Edge-Case Gate (before `P1`/`P2`/`P3` review dispatch):** Every writer verifies before requesting review: (1) trailing path-separator boundaries & fail-closed reparse/symlink checks; (2) locale-independent Windows error/category handling; (3) fail-closed handling on null/empty external payloads (preserving last-known-good caches, never inventing synthetic defaults); (4) `AggregateException`-safe rollback paths on multi-step setup/migration scripts; and (5) WinUI focus visuals & `AutomationPeer` landmarks for UI changes.
- For class `H` and `M` slices, post a short design note (10–20 lines: goal, affected files, approach, risks and tests). Reviewers from complementary profiles (`P1`/`P2`, plus `P3` for UI/product work) check the note before the COO or Solution Architect gives the design Go. **Pre-Approved Plan-Slice Fast-Start (`Class M`):** Slices whose specification, locked paths, and acceptance tests are already fixed inside an approved Spec Kit `plan.md` (such as `#430` or `#510` slices) may start directly on a dedicated task branch with TDD (`Commit 1`: failing acceptance tests -> `Commit 2`: implementation), recording the short design note in the commit / `mobai-swarm-bus` handoff for companion code review without waiting on a separate pre-code prose round (`Class H` safety slices and unspecified features still require Design Go before code). Write a failing acceptance test first for new product behavior. Keep a user-visible feature that spans several slices behind a default-off toggle until complete.

## Deliver small changes to main with review from the start

- While a class `H` or `M` slice grows, the writer commits and pushes small changes early on the task branch (tests first, then implementation) and the cross-family companion reviewer records per-commit findings via `mobai-swarm-bus` (and on the linked GitHub issue when durable audit history is needed).
- Before every push that hands work on, merge the current remote `main` into the slice branch. Resolve conflicts in the writer's worktree and rerun the affected checks. Shared slice branches use merges and regular pushes, never rebase or force-push.
- Once checks, build/test gates, and current SonarCloud findings are green on the task branch, the COO moves the item to **`In review`** and dispatches a fresh independent `P1` (`Opus 5.5`) review (default: Antigravity `Opus 5.5` pool) alongside cross-family `P2`/`P3` verification on the exact head and base (`bus_review_claim`). The writer fixes findings; changes to reviewed content require another independent review at the slice's risk class.
- **Disjoint `main`-Sync Fast-Path (No Redundant LLM Re-Review):** Once a slice already holds both `Fresh Independent P1 PASS` (`Opus 5.5`) and `Cross-Family P2/P3 PASS` on its slice diff, merging `origin/main` strictly to resolve a `BEHIND` branch state does **not** invalidate existing approvals or require another LLM review cycle — provided `origin/main` did not modify any file inside the slice's `LockedAreas` or diff and `git diff origin/main...HEAD` for the slice itself is unchanged (`0` conflict edits in slice files). Re-verify only CI, SonarCloud, and applicable mutation checks on the synced head, and proceed directly to Master merge.
- The COO (or designated Master-merge owner) merges the reviewed task branch head into `main` once required checks, SonarCloud and the applicable mutation checks meet the documented conditions and no blocking findings remain (any new slice commit requires fresh re-review unless it is a pure Disjoint `main`-Sync Fast-Path merge). Close the slice, move Kanban status to **`Done`**, release `bus_locks` after merge, and record `docs/` and `docs/wiki/` impact in the GitHub issue / `mobai-swarm-bus` handoff.
- **Branch & Worktree Hygiene:** Immediately after a task branch merges into `main` (or worktree work is abandoned cleanly), remove the slice worktree, delete its local and remote task branch (`git push origin --delete <branch>`), and run `git fetch --prune`. Only `main` and active slice / standing monitor branches remain checked out or on remote.

## Keep coordination traceable

- **Primary `AISwarmX Swarm Bus`, GitHub Issues & CLI Channels:** The shared local **`AISwarmX Swarm Bus`** (`mobai-swarm-bus` MCP across Claude, Codex, and Antigravity + `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MOBAdev\swarm-bus.db` + Dashboard `Communication` tab + Claude `SessionStart`/`UserPromptSubmit` hook) is the primary real-time coordination bus for cross-tool handoffs (`bus_send`/`bus_inbox`/`bus_ack`), work-queue scheduling (`bus_queue`), review claims (`bus_review_claim`), live overlap-checked path locks (`bus_locks`), and CLI wake-ups (`codex.exe queue`, `Invoke-ClaudeEphemeralWorker.ps1`, `agy.exe`), while GitHub issues remain the durable audit mailbox for design notes, blockers, and final delivery records. Track live slice status on the [MOBAflow GitHub Kanban Board](https://github.com/users/ahuelsmann/projects/1).
- **4 standing Root Head sessions (`AiSwarmX`), runtime task agents auto-archived:** The cockpit operates with **4** standing monitoring/steering sessions: **`AiSwarmX Claude Opus`** (`COO` `P1` / `Opus 5.5` in Claude Code), **`AiSwarmX ChatGPT Codex`** (`Technical Director & CPM` `P2` / `GPT-6.2 Sol` in ChatGPT Codex), **`AiSwarmX Antigravity Gemini`** (`Head of AI Strategy` `P3` / `Gemini 4 Carbon` in Antigravity), and **`AiSwarmX Antigravity Opus`** (`Acting COO & Fresh P1 Opus Orchestrator` `P1` / `Opus 5.5` in Antigravity). Regular intake starts in `AiSwarmX Claude Opus` (`COO`). All task-scoped execution, review and specialist agents (`Writer`, `Companion Reviewer`, `Final Reviewer`, `Head of UI/UX`, `Head of MOBAnews`, `Security`, `DevOps`) are spawned automatically at task runtime with their assigned profile model (`P1`–`P4`) and archived or removed (along with their worktree) immediately upon task completion (`claude rm` / `--no-session-persistence`, `codex archive`, ephemeral `agy --agent` invocations).
- If the same finding survives two failed fix attempts (in companion or final review), or an exchange exceeds ten agent messages on one topic, the COO triggers the circuit breaker and takes the smallest decision needed to the maintainer with a recommendation.
- The **Head of AI Strategy** (`P3` / `AiSwarmX Antigravity Gemini` in Antigravity) guards this `manifest.md`, evaluates orchestration improvements with measurable quality impact, and keeps all sanitized agent, rule and workflow definitions backed up in `ahuelsmann/AISwarmX`.
